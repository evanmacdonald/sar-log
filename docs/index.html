SAR Task Log

A paperless task notebook for BC search and rescue members.

SAR Task Log replaces the paper notebook that scribes carry during a task. It records the task number, subject, scribe, location, a timestamped event timeline, patient vitals, and notes, and exports a PDF at task close that mirrors the paper PEP form.

Everything stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics, no third-party SDKs.
